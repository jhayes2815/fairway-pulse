What the day looks like for your club

Members hit buckets of balls without knowing why a shot curves or loses distance, and a 60-minute lesson feels like a big step. A 25-minute check-up is an easy yes, and it leads to the lesson.

25 min

Per member: warm-up, tracked shots and a personal report.

28–32

Members a day at most, across two bays over 6–7 hours, before breaks.

Your branding

Every report is co-branded to your club, so members stay inside your ecosystem.

You provide

Two adjacent bays or grass tees on your range for the day, and a way to tell members. We send the graphics and the booking link.

We bring and run it

Launch monitors, high-speed cameras, power and trained operators. Set-up takes 30 minutes, with no Wi-Fi or permanent mounting needed.

Your Pro gets the leads

Each report names the member’s top two swing priorities and links straight to your Pro’s booking diary. We don’t replace coaching, we create demand for it.

Choose how the day is paid for

The day and the member reports are the same in every option. Only who pays changes.

Option A: flat day rate

Club pays, members play free

Best for Captain’s Days, open weekends and member retention events.

  • Club books the full day for a fixed fee
  • Every member session is free
  • Full co-branding and Pro Shop lesson funnel
  • No admin burden on club staff
Option B: zero cost to club

Members pay, club earns

No cost to the club. Golfers pay a small fee on the day and the club shares the revenue.

  • Members pay £15–£20 per session
  • 20% revenue share paid back to the club
  • Online booking link shared in your newsletter
  • Minimum booking threshold agreed up front
Option C: local partner backed

A local sponsor pays

A local business, such as a car dealership, wealth manager or estate agent, underwrites the day.

  • Sponsor logo on every AI scorecard
  • Free diagnostic sessions for members
  • Sponsor gets lead capture and bay signage
  • Club hosts a premium event at zero cost
